How a consulting engagement runs.

You always know where the work is, and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Consult & scope

    We sit with you — and, if you want, your client — and map the problem, the constraints and what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Design & architect

    A system design you can take into the room. Reviewed before anyone starts building.

  3. 03

    Build & iterate

    Weekly demos against a visible roadmap. Software, mobile, robotics or AI — same cadence.

  4. 04

    Ship & hand over

    Launch support, full handover, and an optional retainer if you want us to stay on the engagement.
